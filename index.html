Training Load Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

In short: your Garmin data and key stay on your iPhone. The developer has no server, can't see your data, and doesn't collect anything about you.

Who this covers

This policy covers the Training Load app for iPhone, provided by Sergio Gaytan.

What the app handles

When you connect your Garmin account, the app downloads these details from Garmin and stores them on your iPhone:

  • Activities: name, type, start time, duration, distance, training load, training effect, heart rate and time in heart-rate zones.
  • Daily health data: resting heart rate, Body Battery, sleep time, sleep score and heart rate variability (HRV).
  • Profile settings: your Garmin display name and measurement units.
  • Your Garmin Personal API key, which you enter yourself, and a temporary sign-in token the app gets from Garmin and doesn't save.

The app also saves your display choices, such as the selected date range, on your iPhone.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to show your training and recovery information and to calculate figures such as fitness, fatigue, form and readiness. These calculations happen on your iPhone.

Where your data goes

  • The developer doesn't receive it. The app has no developer-run server or user accounts.
  • Garmin: the app connects directly to Garmin over an encrypted connection, sending your key to sign in and asking for your data. Garmin's own privacy policy covers how it handles your data.
  • Nobody else: the app has no advertising or third-party analytics, doesn't sell your data and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

If you've chosen to share diagnostics with app developers on your device, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You control this in your iPhone's settings.

How your data is stored

  • Your key is kept in the iPhone's Keychain and stays on that device.
  • Your synced data is kept in a protected file inside the app, which is left out of device backups. On a new iPhone, you enter a key and sync from Garmin again.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Your data stays on your iPhone until you delete it. To remove it, open the app's Settings and choose Disconnect and delete my data, which deletes your key and synced data from your iPhone. It's best to do this before deleting the app, because iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is removed.

This doesn't affect your Garmin account. You can also revoke your key at any time in Garmin Connect, and manage the data Garmin holds through Garmin.

Children

The app isn't directed at children and requires a Garmin account. The developer doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

The app's figures and training suggestions are for general information only and aren't medical advice.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
